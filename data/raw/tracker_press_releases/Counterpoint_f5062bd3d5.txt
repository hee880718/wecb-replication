Key Features

Smartphone 360

Shipment data and insights across regions and key countries at the monthly level
Sell-through data at country level
Short to long-term forecasts by brand, region and technology
Granular city-level data available for specific markets
Extensive coverage of pricing and promotions data

What is included?

Market Pulse

Monthly sell-in and sell-through data and insights for both smartphone and feature phones. Includes analysis on major industry events and trends.

Market Monitor

Quarterly shipment data by OEMs with breakdowns by ASPs, revenues, connectivity technologies.

Market Outlook

Five-year TAM forecast providing detailed analysis across regions and technologies. Model-level forecasts available for flagships and other popular devices

# Provenance of processed data

How each file in `data/processed/` and `figures/data/` was produced. **Scripted** files are regenerated by a script in this package; **extracted** files were produced by document parsers and spreadsheet extraction from the public originals (Zenodo record 10.5281/zenodo.23638441) and then audited by hand against the originals (three audit rounds plus the 25 October 2026 re-verification; see Appendix F of the paper and `registry/`). Hand-audited values carry their source document in the registry row named in the right-hand column.
